Card 1 · The Big Idea Isaiah 50–52
The lesson, part by part (Original)
The future is bright for the Lord’s people.Isaiah 50–52
  • Israel would spend years as captives because of their own poor choices. Still, the Lord asked them to face what was coming with hope.
  • He promised, “the redeemed of the LORD shall return, and come with singing unto Zion” (Isaiah 51:11).

Talk about it: Which promise in Isaiah 50–52 gives you the most hope for what’s ahead, and why?

Jesus Christ took upon Himself my sins and sorrows.Isaiah 53
  • He was wounded for our transgressions, and with His stripes we are healed.

Talk about it: How has Jesus Christ helped you carry a heavy burden?
