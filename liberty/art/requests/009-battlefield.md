# 009 · The battlefield: camps, a war camp, Lamanite watchtowers, walls and a gate

**Status: done** (phase 2 of finishing free battle). All five pictures were
made with Gemini through Blake's n8n backup: 5 billed to the Merit3D Gemini
account, all usable on the first try.

## What it's for

In free battle you tear down three Lamanite camps and their war camp. Those,
and the robbers' camps in 3 Nephi, were drawn by code: flat triangle tents. The
Lamanites' watchtowers used the Nephite watchtower's picture. Walls were a
ridge of stacked lines that read as a fallen log, and a gate was a small box.

## The pictures

- **`lamanite_camp.png`** (3 × 3): three hide tents with feathers on their
  poles, a fire, a rack of spears and bows, and a drum. It's used for the
  Lamanite camps in free battle and the camp in Antionum (Alma 43:5).
  - Gemini drew it on a white page with a magenta diamond for ground, so
    the white was turned to magenta before the cut-out.
- **`robbers_camp.png`** (3 × 3): red-dyed hide tents, a fire, a rack of
  cimeters and bows, and stolen sacks and jars. It's used for the camps in
  the 3 Nephi missions.
- **`warcamp.png`** (4 × 4): a palisade of sharpened stakes round a painted
  chieftain's tent hung with feathers, smaller tents, a fire, racks and a war
  drum. The game still adds its waving red banner.
- **`lamanite_tower.png`** (2 × 2): lashed unpeeled logs, a hide roof and
  feathers, on an earth mound ringed with stakes.
- **`gate.png`**: a timber gate on a low mound, running lower left to upper
  right. On a wall that runs the other way, it's drawn mirrored.

Gemini drew the three camps from a steeper angle than the game's map, so they
are flattened to 80% of their height and centred on their plots. Flattening
them all the way would have made the tents look stubby.

## Drawn by code

- **Walls:** a bank of earth with sloped sides and a grassy top, about 18
  pixels high. A bank running one way shows its lit side, one running the
  other way its shaded side. Once "Ridges of earth and pickets" is made, a
  row of sharpened pickets runs along the top (Alma 50:1–3).
- **Shadows:** the hall, granary and stables now cast a soft shadow to the
  right, like the older buildings. The shadow is worked out from each
  picture's own shape.
