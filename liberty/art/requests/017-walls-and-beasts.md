# 017 · Walls that level up, the wall guard, and the great beasts

**Status: done.** Seventeen pictures made with Gemini through Blake's n8n
backup (17 billed to the Merit3D Gemini account). Fifteen were usable on the
first try; the stone and King-men ditch sections came back short and deep
and were asked for again as long thin strips. Everything was cut out of the
magenta, de-spilled and scaled: each wall style to one on-screen thickness
(`wall_*.png`, at twice game size), the guard to a unit's 150 px, the beasts
to 240 px wide (three times their size on screen). Cameos for the beasts
and for the new upgrades were made with `mkcameo.py`.

## What it's for

Blake asked for walls you can see grow stronger, and a slow, strong unit.
Decided with him, one question at a time (`liberty/design/evolution.md`,
section 13b): three levels of walls per side, a ditch that slows attackers,
a guard on every fourth piece who casts stones down; a curelom for the
Freemen and a cumom for the King-men (Ether 9:19).

## The pictures

All the wall pieces run from the lower left to the upper right, at the
angle of `gate.png` (the reference for every one of them), with both ends
cut off square so the game can join them.

- **`wall_bank.png`**, **`wall_bank_post.png`**: the bank of earth
  (Alma 48:8), and a round heap where banks meet.
- **`wall_pickets.png`**, **`wall_pickets_post.png`**: timbers as tall as a
  man on the ridge, a frame of sharpened pickets above (Alma 50:2–3), and a
  lashed cluster of pickets for the corners.
- **`wall_stone.png`**, **`wall_stone_post.png`**, **`gate_stone.png`**: a
  bank faced with stone, a stone wall with pickets, a ditch at its foot
  (Alma 48:8, 49:18); a square stone tower for the corners; a stone gateway
  with a bridge over the ditch.
- **`wall_stakes.png`**, **`wall_stakes_post.png`**, **`gate_stakes.png`**:
  the King-men's palisade of sharpened stakes with painted bands; a cluster
  of stakes with a feathered banner; a gate of lashed stakes.
- **`wall_hides.png`**: the palisade hung with hides and painted shields
  (Alma 49:6).
- **`wall_campditch.png`**: the palisade on a bank with a ditch (Alma 55:33).
- **`wallguard.png`**: edited from the spearman: both arms up, a boulder
  over his head, ready to cast it down (Alma 49:22).
- **`curelom.png`**: imagined, since nobody knows what one looked like: a
  shaggy beast between a mastodon and a ground sloth, a war platform on its
  back with two Nephite soldiers.
- **`cumom.png`**: imagined: a huge horned beast like an armored bison, its
  horns capped with wood for butting down walls, a Lamanite driver.

References: `gate.png`, `spearman.png`, `cart.png`.
