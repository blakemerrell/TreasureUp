# 021 · The temple, after the manner of Solomon's: stone and gold, lit, with its lampstand

**Status: done.** Made with Gemini through Blake's n8n backup (executions
13477–13479, three billed to the Merit3D Gemini account). Two layouts were
painted from the temple of request 018 set on a tall magenta canvas: one
with the porch tower at the end of the long house, one with the house across
the back and an open court in front. Shown in the game at their true size,
Blake chose the first, touched up: the porch tower raised about three times
higher and the lampstand made twice as big (edited from the first painting
padded with magenta above, so the tower had room). Cut out, de-spilled at
the outline only, scaled to 150 px a square of plot (4 × 4), with the soft
cast shadow; button picture made with `mkcameo.py`. The drafts
(`liberty/art/incoming/pr93/`) were removed once done.

The temple of request 018 was drawn far larger than its plot and off to one
side of it (its sprite numbers were never updated for the new picture), so
its plot looked empty; the new one stands on its own 4 × 4.

## What it's for

Blake: "Let's make the temple more to the dimensions in the Bible and make
the whole thing more gold with seriphs, huge light lamp stand, alters, etc
... Golden outside to make it really standout?" Decided with him, one
question at a time:

- **Stone and gold, lit within.** Solomon "overlaid the house within with
  pure gold" (1 Kings 6:21–22); outside it was hewn stone carved with
  cherubims, palm trees and open flowers "within and without" (6:29), the
  doors overlaid with gold (6:32). And the Nephites' temple was "not built
  of so many precious things" (2 Nephi 5:16). So: pale stone, gold on the
  carvings, the doors and the roof, and golden light from within.
- **Cherubim, not seraphim**: the temple's winged figures were cherubims
  (1 Kings 6:23–29); seraphims are in Isaiah's vision (Isaiah 6:2).
- **A huge golden lampstand in the court**, lit. Game license: the ten
  golden candlesticks stood inside, "before the oracle" (1 Kings 7:49).
- **The dimensions**: a long, narrow house, "threescore cubits" long,
  "twenty cubits" wide and "thirty cubits" high (1 Kings 6:2), with a porch
  as wide as the house (6:3) rising far above it (2 Chronicles 3:4).
- **The altars and the court**: the altar of brass in the court
  (2 Chronicles 4:1), the two pillars of brass (1 Kings 7:15) and the molten
  sea on twelve oxen (1 Kings 7:23–25).
- Still 4 × 4 on the map, the tallest building on the field.

## The picture

- **`temple.png`**, 4 × 4, on a magenta background, the game's isometric
  angle, light from the upper left: a stepped platform of pale hewn stone in
  a low white court wall; the long, narrow house with carved cherubim, palms
  and flowers picked out in gold, a gold cornice and narrow high windows,
  side chambers along it; at its front the porch tower crowned in gold, its
  gold doors open on warm light; the two bronze pillars with lily-work; in
  the court the great golden lampstand, its seven lamps lit, the bronze
  altar with its steps, and the basin on twelve oxen. White stone, gold and
  bronze only.
- In the game the lampstand's seven lamps flicker with the painted flames
  of request 018.
