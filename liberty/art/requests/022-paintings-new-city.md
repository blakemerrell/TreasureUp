# 022 · The opening paintings: the new palace and the gold temple in the city

**Status: done.** Made with Gemini through Blake's n8n backup (executions
13480–13485, six billed to the Merit3D Gemini account). Each painting was
touched up, not repainted: the game's own `stronghold.png` and `temple.png`
were pasted over the old buildings, Gemini painted them in, and only the
city was taken from its picture. Everything else is the painting of request
019, pixel for pixel (Gemini had changed 0.01–0.15% of each picture outside
the city, and those changes were dropped). Two passes each: the first
painted the buildings in but left them looking like game pieces on thick
white slabs (and, in the Story tile, added two more lampstands and a smoking
altar); the second set them on low stone terraces on the ground, in the
painting's light. In the Free tile the second pass also hazed the hills
beside the spearman's helmet, so its version is faded out over those hills
to leave no seam. The pastes and drafts (`liberty/art/incoming/pr96/`) were
removed once done. Saved as JPEG (quality 80): the title 1376 × 768, the
tiles 900 × 604.

## What it's for

After the city became a white stone palace (request 020) and the temple
stone and gold (request 021), the opening page still showed the old
buildings: a long red-roofed hall in the opening painting, the Story tile
and the Free tile, and a plain white tower for the temple in the opening
painting. Decided with Blake, one question at a time: all three paintings,
and touch up the city only.
