# 016 · The captains, the war-dance ground and the king's court

**Status: done.** Six pictures made with Gemini through Blake's n8n backup
(6 billed to the Merit3D Gemini account): Teancum twice (the first came out
in a Roman cuirass and was asked for again in Nephite dress), Amalickiah,
Ammoron, the war-dance ground and the king's court; all cut out of the
magenta, de-spilled and scaled like the rest (heroes to 160 px tall,
buildings to about 75 px per square of plot). Cameos for all, and for
Helaman (from 006) and Zerahemnah (from 006) besides, made with `mkcameo.py`.

## What it's for

Pick a side, then a captain (`liberty/design/evolution.md`, section 7).
Each captain brings a hero and one gift. The Freemen's are Moroni (005),
Helaman (006) and Teancum; the King-men's Amalickiah, Ammoron and
Zerahemnah (006). The King-men's fourth tier is the king's court, where
cunning is worked; the war-dance ground sends warriors out fierce.

## The pictures

- **`teancum.png`**: edited from the javelin thrower: quilted cotton armor,
  a bronze head-band, a short red cloak, a sling of javelins on his back,
  the javelin raised to throw. "Teancum stole privily into the tent of the
  king" (Alma 51:34).
- **`amalickiah.png`**: edited from Zerahemnah: a tall feathered crown with a
  gold band, a jaguar-skin cloak, a gold collar, a cimeter held low, a sly
  look. "A very subtle man to do evil" (Alma 47:4).
- **`ammoron.png`**: edited from the Lamanite captain: a crown of red and
  black feathers, a long red cloak, a copper breastplate, a sword raised.
  "The brother of Amalickiah was appointed king over the people" (Alma 52:3).
- **`wardance.png`** (3 × 2): a ring of trodden earth round a big fire,
  drums, feathered poles with painted banners, masks on posts.
- **`kingscourt.png`** (3 × 3): a great painted tent with a raised platform
  before it, a throne of hides and jaguar skin under a feathered canopy,
  standards, braziers, a carpet of hides down the steps.
