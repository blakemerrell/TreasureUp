# 018 · The great buildings: the chief judge's hall, the temple, the Rameumptom and the idols

**Status: done.** Made with Gemini through Blake's n8n backup (executions
13439–13450, twelve billed to the Merit3D Gemini account). The judge's hall,
both idols and the six flame frames were right the first time. The
Rameumptom came back once with its braziers outside its square and was asked
for again. The temple took five tries: the first two had a wide court that
left the house smaller than the judge's hall, an edit couldn't make it grow
because the picture's top edge was in the way, and the last was edited from
a draft padded with empty space above (`liberty/art/incoming/pr83/`, removed
once done). Everything was cut out of the magenta, de-spilled at the outline
only (the Rameumptom's red floor isn't magenta), scaled to 150 px a square of
plot, and given the soft cast shadow the other buildings have. Button
pictures made with `mkcameo.py`.

## What it's for

Blake's play-test: Zarahemla's huge red pyramid, the start building, stood
taller than the temple. He asked for a beautiful, large temple like
Solomon's, and for the King-men a Rameumptom and huge idols. Decided with him
one question at a time (`liberty/design/evolution.md`, section 13c).

## The pictures

All at the game's isometric angle, seen from above at the front corner, the
plot a diamond twice as wide as tall, light from the upper left.

- **`stronghold.png`** (replaced in request 020 by a white stone palace that matches the other buildings), the chief judge's hall at Zarahemla (Alma 50:39),
  4 × 4: a long, low hall of tan stone and dark timber under red tiles, a
  porch of square pillars, a roofed seat of judgment on a dais before it,
  trees, all in a yard behind a bank of earth and pickets. Reference:
  `hall.png`.
- **`temple.png`** (replaced in request 021: stone and gold, its lampstand in the court), the temple "after the manner of the temple of Solomon"
  (2 Nephi 5:16), 4 × 4: white limestone and gold on a pale platform; a porch
  tower that rises far above the house (2 Chronicles 3:4), gold doors, two
  pillars of brass with lily capitals (1 Kings 7:15), a wide stair, an altar,
  and the basin on twelve oxen (1 Kings 7:23–25). Reference: the old
  `temple.png`, then the drafts.
- **`rameumptom.png`**, the Zoramites' holy stand (Alma 31:13, 21), 4 × 4: a
  court of polished red and dark stone inside a colonnade hung with crimson
  and gold, four bronze braziers, and in the middle a slender tower with a
  stair winding up to a railed top for one man, a priest there with his arms
  raised. Reference: `kingscourt.png` (now retired).
- **`idol_jaguar.png`** and **`idol_warrior.png`**, 3 × 3: towering stone
  idols on stepped bases, one seated with a jaguar's face and a feathered
  headdress, one standing with a stone shield and an obsidian-edged club;
  fruit, maize and incense at their feet. The game alternates the two.
- **`flames.png`**: six frames of one flame on black, flipped like a
  flipbook and added as light. They burn in the Rameumptom's braziers (and in
  the "Fire and Frames" demo Blake asked for).

Not used: no feathered serpent anywhere, since some see Christ in that
figure.

## What "done" looks like

At normal zoom the temple is the tallest thing on the field, the judge's hall
is plainly a seat of government and lower than it, the Rameumptom's stand
rises over its colonnade with fire in its braziers, and an idol reads as a
huge statue, not a hut.
