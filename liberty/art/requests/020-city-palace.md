# 020 · The city: the chief judge's palace, in white stone

**Status: done.** Made with Gemini through Blake's n8n backup (executions
13475 and 13476, two billed to the Merit3D Gemini account), with the hall of
the captains (`hall.png`) as the reference for the look. Two versions were
painted at once and shown to Blake in the game; he chose the first. It was
cut out of the magenta, de-spilled at the outline only, scaled to 150 px a
square of plot (4 × 4) and given the soft cast shadow the other buildings
have. Button picture made with `mkcameo.py`.

## What it's for

Blake, playing Out of the Wilderness: "Need a different art object for this.
Does not seem to fit the style of the area." The judge's hall from request
018 was a Roman-looking villa (red tile roof, tan stone columns, a fenced
garden on a bank of earth), while every other Nephite building is white
plaster and pale limestone with flat or thatched roofs on stepped stone
platforms. Decided with him: a white stone palace.

## The picture

- **`stronghold.png`**, 4 × 4, the palace of the chief judge, the
  judgment-seat (Alma 50:39), used wherever the Nephites' city stands
  (Zarahemla, Jershon and Manti in the stories; the city planted with the
  standard in a skirmish): a broad stepped platform of pale limestone with a
  wide stair; a colonnaded palace of white plaster with gold trim and a
  smaller upper storey; at the foot of the stair a stone dais with the seat
  of judgment under a cream canopy, and beside it the title of liberty on its
  pole (Alma 46:12–13). Low and wide: grand, but lower than the temple.

Not used: the courtyard version (two wings round an open court with a grand
stair), which looked closer to the hall of the captains.

The opening page's paintings (request 019) still show the old red-roofed
hall behind Moroni and on the Story tile.
