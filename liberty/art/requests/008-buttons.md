# 008 · A picture on every button

**Status: done** (with the warm command panel, phase 1 of finishing free battle).

## What it's for

The command panel's buttons are now square picture tiles, with the name on
the picture and the cost underneath. Before, half of them had no picture,
and four old ones were cut from the wrong part of a sheet:

- the "stripling" button showed the armory's forge;
- the "lamanite" button showed a stripling;
- the watchtower and armory buttons were slivers.

## Where each picture came from

All are 120 × 120 on a warm brown ground with a gold frame, made with the same
cut-out and framing as `cameo_worker.png`:

- **From the game's own pictures** (no new art):
  - a head-and-shoulders crop for `cameo_spearman`, `cameo_stripling`,
    `cameo_moroni` and `cameo_lamanite`;
  - the whole picture for `cameo_farm`, `cameo_storehouse`,
    `cameo_barracks`, `cameo_tower`, `cameo_armory`, `cameo_stronghold` and
    `cameo_cart`.
- **Painted by Gemini through Blake's n8n backup** (6 pictures billed to the
  Merit3D Gemini account, all usable on the first try):
  - `cameo_wall`: a ridge of earth with pickets;
  - `cameo_gate`: a timber gate in the ridge;
  - `cameo_breastplates`: a bronze breastplate, shield and arm-shields;
  - `cameo_cimeters`: a sword and a cimeter crossed;
  - `cameo_pickets`: a mound of earth with sharpened pickets;
  - `cameo_armor`: a rack of spears and a bow, with a shield and breastplate.
    It's for Gidgiddoni's "weapons, armor and shields".

Stop, Let go, Done and Cancel have drawn signs instead of pictures.
