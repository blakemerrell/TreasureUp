# 011 · The temple

**Status: done.** One picture made with Gemini through Blake's n8n backup,
1 billed to the Merit3D Gemini account, usable on the first try.

## What it's for

Blake asked for stone "to make temples that give great power for Jehovah
worship", and chose what the temple does: your people near it are made
whole, the council comes back sooner and gives double, and miracles are
worked from it. It needs the hall of the captains and costs mostly stone.

## What the verses say

Nephi built a temple "after the manner of the temple of Solomon"
(2 Nephi 5:16). King Benjamin had a tower built by the temple so the people
could hear him (Mosiah 2:7). The miracles each have their own verse: the
pillar of fire and the cloud of darkness (Helaman 5:23–28), the earthquake
that opened the prison (Alma 14:27), the deep sleep of the guards
(Alma 55:16), enemies turned on each other (Judges 7:22), "O Lord, have mercy"
(Alma 2:30), and Nephi shaking his brothers (1 Nephi 17:54).

## The picture

`temple.png`, 3 × 3 squares, edited from `hall.png` so the angle and light
match: a tall stepped platform of white plastered limestone, three terraces
with red and deep-blue painted bands, a broad straight stair up the
front-left face, a sanctuary on top with white walls, a dark timber door
and a steep thatched roof, two stone braziers with thin smoke at the top of
the stair, and a slim wooden speaking tower with a railed platform at the
right-hand corner of the top terrace, like King Benjamin's.

Cut out, despilled, given the same soft cast shadow as the other buildings,
and scaled to 440 pixels wide. Its stair pokes out past the platform's
diamond, so the picture is a little wider than the plot; it's set on the
plot by its front corner like the others. The Build button
(`cameo_temple.png`) is the whole picture in the gold frame.

## Drawn by code

The miracle buttons are drawn signs, like Stop and Cancel: a flame, a
cloud, a cracked line, two z's, crossed strokes, a heart and a lightning
bolt. The miracles themselves are drawn on the ground: a ring of flames, a
dark cloud, cracks that fade, drifting z's, a red dashed ring, a soft light
over your people, and a bolt.
