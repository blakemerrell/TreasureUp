# 005 · The heroes: Lehi, Gidgiddoni and Helaman

**Status: done.** All three (`lehi.png`, `gidgiddoni.png`, `helaman.png`) are in the game.

## What it's for

Heroes lead the army: each has a gold ring on the map and strengthens the
soldiers near him. Today Lehi (in the Moroni mission) and Gidgiddoni (in the
3 Nephi missions) are both drawn with Moroni's picture, so you can't tell
which captain is which. Helaman isn't in a mission yet; his picture will be
ready for the war chapters with his stripling warriors. On the map a hero is
about 48 pixels tall, a little bigger than a soldier, so each needs **one
colour and one thing in his hands** that read at a glance.

## What the verses say

- **Lehi:** "a man who had been with Moroni in the more part of all his
  battles; and he was a man like unto Moroni" (Alma 53:2).
- **Gidgiddoni:** "the chiefest among all the chief captains and the great
  commander of all the armies of the Nephites" (3 Nephi 3:18); "this
  Gidgiddoni was a great prophet among them" (3 Nephi 3:19).
- **Helaman:** "Helaman did march at the head of his two thousand stripling
  soldiers" (Alma 53:22).

## The pictures

Start from `liberty/assets/moroni.png` for all three (edit it, don't draw
from scratch), so they keep his size, viewing angle, light from the upper
left and painted look, and his rank: bronze armor and a cloak. **Turn each
toward the viewer's left, like Moroni.** Moroni's own marks are the **red**
cloak and crest and the banner, so none of these three has red or a banner.

1. **`lehi.png`, Lehi.** Like Moroni, as the verse says, but a **deep blue
   cloak**, a bronze helmet with a white crest, a sword in his right hand
   and a round bronze shield on his left arm. A man in his prime.
2. **`gidgiddoni.png`, Gidgiddoni.** Older, with a short grey beard, a
   commander and a prophet: bronze breastplate under a long **white mantle**,
   no helmet (a plain bronze band on his brow), a tall wooden **staff** in his
   right hand and a sword at his side.
3. **`helaman.png`, Helaman.** A grown man, older than his young soldiers,
   in **gold** armor like `stripling.png`'s (gold breastplate and round gold
   shield, white tunic), with a short **green** cloak and a sword raised to
   lead them forward.

## Style notes

Soft painted shading and thin outlines, like `spearman.png` and your
round-4 swordsman. Flat magenta (#FF00FF) background, one figure each,
nothing cropped, feet fully in the frame (see GEMINI.md). **Check each faces
the viewer's left before you reply:** two of the last four came back facing
right.

## What "done" looks like

At 48 pixels tall next to Moroni, you can tell Lehi (blue cloak, shield),
Gidgiddoni (white mantle, staff) and Helaman (gold armor, green cloak) at a
glance, and all face left.
