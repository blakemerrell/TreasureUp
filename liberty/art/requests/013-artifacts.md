# 013 · Treasures: five artifacts and a Jaredite ruin

**Status: done.** Six pictures made with Gemini through Blake's n8n backup,
6 billed to the Merit3D Gemini account, all usable on the first try.

## What it's for

Blake asked for artifacts "like the Liahona" that give a general insight,
and chose to find them both in ruins on the map and through the council.
Limhi's men found a land "covered with ruins of buildings of every kind"
(Mosiah 8:8) and in it plates, breastplates and swords (8:9–11): so three
Jaredite ruins stand on the free-battle map, and the council's gifts grow
with right answers in a row.

## The pictures

The five artifacts were drawn as objects on magenta, using an existing
picture only for its style and light, then cut out and set in the gold
frame like the research buttons. They show in the panel once held.

- **`cameo_sword.png`**: the sword of Laban, "the hilt thereof was of pure
  gold" (1 Nephi 4:9), with its scabbard.
- **`cameo_liahona.png`**: "a round ball of curious workmanship; and it was
  of fine brass" (1 Nephi 16:10), its two spindles seen through an opening.
- **`cameo_breastplate.png`**: a Jaredite breastplate, "large, and … of
  brass and of copper" (Mosiah 8:10), greened with age.
- **`cameo_plates.png`**: the brass plates, bound with rings, engraved.
- **`cameo_interpreters.png`**: "two stones which were fastened into the
  two rims of a bow" (Mosiah 28:13), clear crystal in silver.
- **`ruin.png`**: the ruin on the map, 2 × 2 squares: tumbled cut blocks,
  a fallen fluted pillar, moss, and a stone box with its lid ajar and a
  golden glint. Edited from the Lamanite camp for the angle. Drawn at
  128 px across on the plot like the other buildings, on ground turned to
  ruin-tiles around it.

## Drawn by code

The sword bearer's mark, a small gold sword above the head beside any
chevrons; the Liahona's brass pointer at the edge of the view.
