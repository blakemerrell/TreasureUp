# 012 · The spy, and three upgrades at the armory

**Status: done.** Four pictures made with Gemini through Blake's n8n backup,
4 billed to the Merit3D Gemini account, all usable on the first try.

## What it's for

Blake's list after the temple: ways to upgrade units, "steel bow vs wood
bow", "ladders to climb over walls", and spies. These are the pictures for
the barracks' new unit and the armory's three new upgrades.

## What the verses say

- **Spies:** "Moroni sent spies into the wilderness to watch their camp"
  (Alma 43:23); Alma did the same (Alma 2:21).
- **Bows of fine steel:** Nephi's bow "was made of fine steel"
  (1 Nephi 16:18). It broke, and he made one of wood; so wood to steel is a
  true upgrade.
- **Thick clothing:** Moroni's people "were dressed with thick clothing"
  (Alma 43:19).
- **Ladders and cords:** Moroni's men took Nephihah by night with "strong
  cords and ladders, to be let down from the top of the wall" (Alma 62:21).
  The Lamanites, for their part, "began to dig down their banks of earth"
  and climb, and "were swept off by the stones and arrows" (Alma 49:22).

## The pictures

- **`spy.png`**: edited from `worker.png` so the scale and angle match. A
  lean young Nephite in a short dun tunic and a hooded travelling cloak
  pushed back, a water skin and pouch on his belt, a walking staff, one
  hand shading his eyes. 150 px tall like the worker; drawn 20 × 40 on
  screen. Button: `cameo_spy.png`, a head-and-shoulders crop.
- **`cameo_bows.png`**: a recurved bow of polished steel with a leather
  grip, three bronze-headed arrows crossed beside it.
- **`cameo_clothing.png`**: a quilted cotton war tunic, cream with blue
  stitching and borders, spread flat with a woven belt across it.
- **`cameo_ladders.png`**: a scaling ladder of lashed poles, a coil of rope
  with a grappling hook at its foot.

The three objects were drawn on magenta using an existing picture only for
its style and light, then cut out and set in the gold frame like the other
research buttons.
