# 002 · The Gadianton robbers

**Status: done.** All three (pull request #53) are in the game:
`liberty/assets/robber.png`, `robber_archer.png` and `robber_chief.png`
(Giddianhi and Zemnarihah).

## What it's for

Robbers, robber archers and the robber chiefs (Giddianhi and Zemnarihah) are
all drawn with the Lamanite's picture today, so in the raid skirmish and in
3 Nephi you can't tell a robber from a Lamanite. On the map each is about
44 pixels tall, so the difference has to show at that size, not just up
close.

## What the verse says

"They were girded about after the manner of robbers; and they had a
lamb-skin about their loins, and they were dyed in blood, and their heads
were shorn, and they had head-plates upon them; and great and terrible was the
appearance of the armies of Giddianhi, because of their armor" (3 Nephi 4:7).

For a child's game: the dye is a dark red-brown body paint, **not blood**.

## The pictures

Start from `liberty/assets/lamanite.png` for all three (edit it, don't draw
from scratch), so they keep its size, pose, viewing angle (three quarters,
turned toward the viewer's left), light from the upper left and painted look.
Then change him:

1. **`robber.png`, the robber.** Head shorn (shaved), with a metal
   head-plate strapped over the crown. A white woolly lamb-skin wrapped about
   the loins, the most visible difference from the Lamanite's brown
   loincloth. Arms and chest painted dark red-brown. A dark leather strap
   across the chest. A curved sword (a cimeter) in his hand. No feathers, no
   long hair.
2. **`robber_archer.png`, the robber archer.** The same robber with a bow
   held ready and a quiver on his back, no sword, and lighter build.
3. **`robber_chief.png`, the chief.** The same look made grander: a bronze
   head-plate with a ridge, a bronze breastplate over the red-brown paint, a
   short dark cloak, the white lamb-skin, a cimeter. Taller and more
   commanding, still not scary for a child.

## Style notes from the first round

Match `lamanite.png`'s soft painted shading. Keep outlines thin: round one
came back a little brighter and more heavily outlined than the game's art.
Flat magenta (#FF00FF) background, one figure each, nothing cropped (see
GEMINI.md).

## What "done" looks like

At 44 pixels tall, next to a Lamanite, you can tell which is the robber
(bare shaved head with a plate, white lamb-skin, no feathers), which robber
carries a bow, and which is the chief.

## What came back

Three pictures in a softer painted style than round one, closer to the
game's. The white lamb-skin and the head-plates make a robber easy to tell
from a Lamanite on the map; at normal zoom it's the head and the white that
tell them apart. Claude cut each out of its magenta, cropped it to the figure,
scaled it to 150 pixels tall and set it by its feet, since the sword and the
bow make the pictures lopsided.
